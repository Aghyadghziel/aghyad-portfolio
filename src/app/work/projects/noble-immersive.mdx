## The brief

A self-initiated concept, built at SIMA Studio for a fictional luxury fragrance house that wants its website to feel like a campaign rather than a catalogue.

Perfume sells through the senses, and a screen has none of them. The question was whether depth, warmth and craft can be carried by light and motion instead.

## What I built

**One continuous film.** The bottle arrives, the brand statement lands, oud, saffron and amber gather around it, and the same bottle becomes the product at the end. Chapters are driven by a single scroll timeline rather than a stack of separate animations, so the story never stutters between sections.

**Ingredient atmospheres.** Choosing an ingredient changes the lighting, the colour grade and the particles around the bottle — the interface argument being that you understand a fragrance note faster by seeing its temperature than by reading it.

**A 360° showroom.** On the product page the bottle can be dragged, turned and zoomed, with the commerce layer attached to the same object the story has been circling for the whole page.

**Bilingual.** Arabic and English, with type picked per script so neither reads as the translated one.

## Notes

This is a concept: the house, the fragrances and the prices are invented, and it is labelled that way wherever it appears. What is real is the engineering — the scroll timeline, the lighting states and the product viewer are the same techniques used on client work.
