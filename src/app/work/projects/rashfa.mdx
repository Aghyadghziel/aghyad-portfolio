## The brief

A self-initiated concept, built at SIMA Studio for a fictional specialty coffee café.

Cafés compete on atmosphere, so the site had to carry the feeling of the place — light, texture, calm — while loading instantly for someone standing outside deciding whether to come in.

## What I built

**An art direction with a point of view.** Midday sun and long shadows rather than the usual soft, warm café photography, so the brand looks like itself and not like every other roaster.

**A pinned drinks chapter.** Scrolling rolls a counter and wipes in each signature drink in place, turning a menu list into a sequence.

**Open-now status.** The header states whether the café is open, computed from opening hours in local time rather than a static line that goes stale.

**A mobile action bar.** WhatsApp, directions and call, always within thumb reach — the three things anyone actually wants from a café site on a phone.

**Astro, deliberately.** The page is static HTML with motion added only where it earns its place, which is why it holds up on a weak connection.

## Notes

A concept project: the café, its menu and its location are invented and labelled as such. It exists to show art direction and motion on a fast, content-led site rather than a heavy one.
